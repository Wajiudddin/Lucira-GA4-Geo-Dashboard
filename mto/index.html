OPERATING MODEL · ORIENTATION

Three journeys, one controlled operating model

The source changes. The control backbone does not.

An MTO order moves through creation, validation, vendor production, inward, quality check, tagging, invoicing and final customer fulfilment. Online, store and customer orders enter through different channels, then converge around production control and quality assurance.

ONLINE MTO

Customer → Shopify

Shopify → Ornaverse → Product Verification (Product Team) → Vendor → Production → Inward → Quality Check → Tagging → Invoice → Logistics → Customer

FINAL MOVEMENT: LOGISTICS
STORE MTO

Customer → Store

Store → Ornaverse → Product Verification (Product Team) → Vendor → Production → Inward → Quality Check → Tagging → Invoice → Store → Customer Pickup

FINAL MOVEMENT: STORE PICKUP
CUSTOMER ORDER · CUSTOM DESIGN

Customer order → Design

Design → CAD → Customer Approval → SKU Generation → Ornaverse → Vendor → Production → Inward → Quality Check → Tagging → Invoice → Store → Customer Pickup

CORE PROCESS · END-TO-END CONTROL FLOW

The full MTO control flow

Every order advances through a defined sequence. A QC failure is a controlled loop, not a dead end.

01

Order received → created → validated

Capture the order, punch it into the relevant system and hold incomplete or incorrect details for correction. Customer orders first complete Design → CAD → Customer approval → SKU generation.

02

Ornaverse → product verification (Online / Store MTO)

Product Team verifies the product details before the order is released to the vendor.

03

Vendor allocation → order placement → production

Assign the appropriate vendor, formally place the order and complete vendor production.

04

Product received → inward

Product received from the vendor is inwarded before inspection.

05

Quality check (QC)

Inspect the inwarded product before any tagging or invoice activity.

06

QC PASS → tagging → invoice

Only a passed product continues to operational completion.

07

QC FAIL → Mumbai in-house vendor → rework / correction → product returns → re-QC

The order returns to the control point and continues only after successful re-QC.

08

Final fulfilment

Route to customer delivery for Online MTO or to the originating store for customer pickup.

Decision rule: QC failure must not move directly to tagging or invoicing.

JOURNEY C · CUSTOMER ORDER (CUSTOM DESIGN)

Customer order — from custom design to pickup

A design-led order: the customer approves the design, and the SKU is generated, before the order enters Ornaverse.

01

Customer order

Customer places a custom MTO requirement with the store / sales team.

02

Design

Design is prepared as per the customer requirement.

03

CAD

CAD is created from the design.

04

Customer approval

Design and CAD are shared with the customer and approved before release. No approval, no order.

05

SKU generation

SKU is generated for the approved design.

06

Ornaverse → vendor → production

Approved order is created and validated in Ornaverse, then sent to the appropriate vendor. Standard production TAT: approximately 15 Days.

07

Inward → quality check → tagging → invoice

Product received from vendor and inwarded, inspected by QC, then tagged and invoiced after QC pass.

08

Return to originating store

Product is sent to the respective store, not directly to the customer.

09

Customer pickup

Customer visits the store and collects the completed product.

Exception path: QC fail → Mumbai in-house vendor → rework → product returns → re-QC. Continue only after QC pass.

QUALITY CONTROL · EXCEPTION LOOP

QC failure must resolve through rework and re-QC

Quality control is a gate: a failed product returns to correction and cannot proceed to tagging or invoicing until it passes again.

01

Product received & inward

Vendor product is received and inwarded.

02

Quality check

QC reviews the inwarded product.

03

QC PASS

Continue to tagging.

04

QC FAIL

Send to Mumbai in-house vendor.

05

Rework / correction

Complete the required correction.

06

Product returns

Receive the corrected product again.

RE-QC

Inspect again

Only after PASS does the normal flow resume.

NORMAL FLOW
Tagging → Invoice
Do not bypass the quality gate.
Control rule: QC FAIL → rework / correction → product returns → re-QC → QC PASS.
