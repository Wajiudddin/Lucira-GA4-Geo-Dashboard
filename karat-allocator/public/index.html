Guide · Lucira Jewelry

How It Works

A plain-English guide to each tab: what it shows, where the numbers come from, and how they are worked out.

The big picture
  1. Karat Allocator — turns the store plan into exact piece counts for the quantity you choose.
  2. Slab Analysis — shows what customers look at (GA4) and what they buy (sales), by city. These shares shape several Karat Allocator tables.
  3. SKU Lists — picks real SKUs to fill the plan.
  4. Store Comparison — checks what each store really holds against the plan.

Nothing is guessed. If a value is missing it shows "Not Available"; if it does not apply to that item it shows "Not Applicable".

Words used on this dashboard
  • Plan target — how many pieces the plan says a store should hold.
  • In store — pieces physically on hand now.
  • Gap — in store − plan target. Negative = short, positive = excess.
  • Merged % — the average of a value's share of GA4 add-to-cart and its share of sales.
  • Rounding — every table that splits a quantity rounds each cell down, then hands the leftover pieces to the biggest fractions, so totals always match exactly.
💍 Karat Allocator
Source: store based plan.xlsx
  • Price band × category and subcategory — every cell in the plan has a fixed % split across 9kt / 14kt / 18kt. That % × your quantity gives the pieces.
  • Ring Size, Bracelet Size, Karat, Metal Color — the Merged % of each value (GA4 add-to-cart share and sales share, averaged) is applied to the quantity being divided.
  • Collection — 40% of the plan. Five collections have fixed shares (Hexa 2%, Portuguese 2%, On The Move 3.6%, Nakshatra 2.4%, Eterna 7%); the rest of the 40% is split across all other collections by Merged %.
  • Bestseller — the Bestseller target (a % of the quantity, 8% by default) is split across categories by their share of GA4 Bestseller SKUs.
  • City filter — tick one city or several (their numbers are added together). It reshapes the Merged % tables.
📊 Slab Analysis
Source: GA4 + the ERP sales ledger
  • GA4 Count — distinct SKU variants that were added to cart, not raw clicks.
  • Sales Qty — pieces actually sold, all channels.
  • City filter — shows only that city (or those cities). Only about half of sales pieces have a city recorded; the rest stay under "Not Available" instead of being assigned to a city.
🏬 Store Comparison
Source: Live_inventory, read live (refreshed every 10 minutes)
  • Stores — PN1 Pune, PV1 Paschim Vihar, LPN Lajpat Nagar, N18 Noida, BO1 Borivali, FCS HWI Ventures (Chembur). Head-office / warehouse stock and the Repair location are not counted. Tick several stores to add them up.
  • Plan — the Karat Allocator's targets (same percentages and rounding) for the quantity picked on this tab, per store (several stores multiply it). The quantity here is separate from the Karat Allocator tab's.
  • In store — each piece is counted by Live_inventory's own category and karat, grouped into the 7 plan categories: Rings; Earrings (incl. Pendent, Men's and Necklace earrings); Pendants + Charms; Necklaces; Mangalsutra / Tanmaniya (incl. Chain); Bracelets (incl. Stiff Bracelets and SBR); Nose Pin. Everything else, mainly gold coins and silver jewellery, is listed by name under "Outside the plan".
  • Silver and platinum — silver and platinum jewellery are never counted in a plan category; they appear under "Outside the plan", for example "Bracelets · silver" or "Chain · platinum".
  • Karat columns — the 9kt / 14kt / 18kt columns compare each karat with its own target.
  • Metal Color and Bestseller tables — count gold pieces in the 7 plan categories. Collection counts every piece that has that collection, inside or outside the plan categories.
  • Plan for those three tables — calculated like the Karat Allocator's, from the Slab Analysis data for the store's own cities (table below). If several stores are ticked, each uses its own cities and the results are added. The "Plan city basis" switch can use All Cities instead.

A store can use several cities; their GA4 and sales are added together before the shares are worked out.

🏷️ SKU Lists
Source: ERP catalog, Live_inventory, GA4 and sales
  • One row per exact SKU — different sizes, karats or metal colors are different SKUs and are never merged.
  • Price band — the same 11 bands as the Karat Allocator (0k-5k up to 225k+), applied to the GA4 price.
  • Store availability and Current Qty — from Live_inventory, per exact SKU per store. Ageing is the days since the oldest piece of that SKU was transferred into the store (HWI Ventures has no transfer record, so no ageing).
  • PDP Views, ATC, Checkouts — lifetime GA4 totals for the product, shown on every size of it. ATC Ratio = ATC ÷ PDP Views × 100. Lifetime Sales = pieces sold across Store, Online and B2B.
  • Solitaire — a piece is Solitaire if any verified diamond on it is over 0.25ct. Gender comes from sales data, otherwise from catalog tags, and defaults to Women's.

How the pick list is built (it rebuilds when the quantity, Bestseller target or city changes):

  1. Each plan category gets its exact count, split into Price Band × Karat buckets using the Karat Allocator's own table.
  2. Each bucket's Bestseller share is filled first with real Bestseller SKUs, then the rest with other SKUs.
  3. Candidates are ranked: Sales → Checkouts → ATC % → ATC → PDP views (city-wise when a city is picked). Ties go to the SKU in more stores, then higher Current Qty, then SKU code.
  4. Picks also steer toward the Metal Color and Collection targets, in two passes. This changes which SKUs are chosen, never how many.
  5. A SKU is never picked twice, and only one SKU per design (style code).
  6. If a bucket runs short, it is topped up from the same karat in other bands, then any band in the same category, then by relaxing the one-per-design rule. Nothing is taken from another category. Any gap is reported in the "Shortfall" tile.

Suggested Size (rings and bracelets): the nearest real size of the same design that has no stock anywhere. Filters, search and sorting only change what is shown and exported, never which SKUs were generated.

How fresh is the data?
  • Live: store stock on the Store Comparison tab (refreshed every 10 minutes, or press "Refresh now").
  • Snapshots, updated when the dashboard is redeployed: the SKU catalog, GA4 numbers, sales, store ageing and city data.
If a number ever looks wrong, this page is the quickest way to check how it is calculated.
