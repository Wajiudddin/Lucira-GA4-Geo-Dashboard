Store Performance & Customer Journey Analytics

How to read this page

A quick guide. The details are further down.

  • Dashboard tab: headline cards, charts, and one table with every metric for Yesterday, Last Week and Last Month. Use the Store filter to look at one store, several, or all.
  • Store Comparison tab: stores down the side, 28 columns across: Inventory, Sq Ft, Number of Staff, Walkins (total, new, repeat), Meetings Raised, Customers (total, new, repeat), Orders, Online Influenced Orders, Qty, Revenue, Purchase Cost, Margin, Margin %, ATV, New and Repeat Revenue, New and Repeat ATV, Revenue Per Staff, Margin / Sq Ft, Scans, Scans Per Walkin, Google Rating and Reviews. Click a heading to rank the stores, or an Orders number to open the bills behind it.
  • City Wise tab: one row per city (Mumbai, Pune, Delhi, Bangalore) with its number of stores, City Sessions, City Deals, Meetings Raised, Visited From Meetings, Order Conversion, Spend and the three cost figures. A city is the sum of its stores: Mumbai is Borivali and Chembur, Delhi is Paschim Vihar, Lajpat Nagar and Noida, Bangalore is Malleshwaram.
  • Each tab has its own filters. The period, custom dates and store choice you set on one tab do not change the other tabs.
  • Freshness: data is loaded when you open the page and kept for 15 minutes. Press Refresh to reload. If one source is down, only the metrics that use it show "Data unavailable".

Rules that change the numbers

The decisions worth knowing before you compare figures.

  • Free items are ignored. A bill whose total net sale is ₹0 is not an order. An item with a net sale of ₹0 (for example a free gold coin on a paid bill) is not counted in Qty, Purchase, Sales Amount or Margin either. Cancelled bills still count as orders; they are marked in red in the bill list.
  • Made-to-order (MTO) pieces. On the Store Comparison tab an MTO piece counts only once it is billed: on the day its final RTS bill was made, at the RTS bill amount (the price can change between order and bill). Pre-order (RPO) bills are not counted there. The Dashboard tab still counts the pre-order bill.
  • Walk-ins come from the ERP walk-in log (Lucira_Prod.customer_walkin, rows marked "Store Walkin"; its bill, receipt and enquiry entries are not visits). A person with a valid mobile counts once per store per day.
  • Mumbai and Delhi sessions are shared. Website sessions can't tell Borivali from Chembur, or Paschim Vihar from Lajpat Nagar, so each pair gets half of its city's sessions. In City Wise the halves add back to the city total, and Delhi includes Noida.
  • Deals are an estimate. Zoho deals have no store, so each deal goes to a store by the customer's city: Mumbai is split 50/50 between Borivali and Chembur; Central, South and South West Delhi go to Lajpat Nagar; North, North West and West Delhi to Paschim Vihar; East Delhi and Noida to Noida.
  • Margin = Sales Amount − Purchase Amount (net sale is not used), only for items that have both. Sales Amount is the POS invoice amount after discount.
  • Purchase cost is looked up in this order: the purchase invoice (GRN), then the consignment-in (APU), then, if the piece was sold under a different item line number than it was bought under (for example an MTO piece), the same lookup under the line number of the stock piece allocated to it. The Orders pop-up shows the GRN or APU number and both line numbers.
  • Fixed values: Number of Staff and store area (sq ft) are set by the business, not read from a system. Chembur 5 staff / 650 sq ft, Borivali 5 / 513, Pune 7 / 1,500, Paschim Vihar 4 / 1,184, Noida 6 / 1,600, Lajpat Nagar 5 / 690, Malleshwaram 5 / 1,350.
  • Spend is partial. Only ad campaigns whose name contains a store or city are counted. Pan-India campaigns are left out, so the cost figures are lower-bound estimates.

Metrics in plain words

Website traffic

City SessionsAll website visits from the city (a visit = one user's session). Counted for Mumbai, Delhi, Pune, Noida and Bengaluru only.
Paid SessionsVisits from Google ads (source Google, not organic) or Facebook / Instagram ads.
Retargeting SessionsVisits from retargeting ads (Criteo / RTB).
Communication SessionsVisits from our own WhatsApp, push, SMS or email messages. Not part of Paid.

The three channels do not add up to City Sessions. The rest is organic search, direct visits with no source, other referral sites and ad sources the Paid rule does not cover.

Leads and store visits

DealsDeals created in Zoho, given to a store by the customer's city (see the rules above).
Meetings RaisedMeetings created in Zoho, counted on the day they were raised. The store is the meeting owner's store, or the one named in the Venue. A blank Venue counts in All Stores only; non-store venues (HO, Video Call) are ignored.
Visited From Meetings (City Wise)Of the meetings raised in the period, the ones where the customer came to the store. From 22 Sep 2026 this is the Show mark the store team sets on the meeting in Zoho. For earlier meetings, a store visit by the same phone number after the meeting was raised counts.
Order Conversion (City Wise)Of the meetings raised in the period, the ones that turned into an order, shown with the share of Meetings Raised. From 22 Sep 2026 this is the Converted mark in Zoho. For earlier meetings, a bill by the visiting customer on the visit day or later counts.
WalkinsStore visits, on the day they happened. Malleshwaram has none recorded.
New / Repeat WalkinsA phone number's first visit in the log (which starts Oct 2025) is New; every later visit is Repeat. A visit with no phone number counts as New.
Walkins From Meetings (Dashboard tab)Store visits by customers who had a meeting raised before the visit, matched by phone number, counted once on the visit day.

Sales

OrdersIn-store bills (online orders excluded). Click the number on the Store Comparison tab to see every bill, piece by piece.
Online Influenced Orders / %Orders where the buyer already had a Zoho deal, or had signed up on the website (email "subscribed"), before the order. The % is that count divided by Orders. Matched on mobile number or email.
RevenueDashboard tab: net sale of the bills. Store Comparison tab: the Sales Amount (see Margin below), so it can be lower than the real net sale.
QtyPieces sold.
Customers, New / RepeatDifferent customers who bought, split by the sales record's New / Repeat tag. Customers = New + Repeat. A customer buying on two days counts on each.
New / Repeat RevenueRevenue split the same way.
ATV, New / Repeat ATVAverage value of a bill: Revenue divided by Orders (New ATV by New Orders, Repeat ATV by Repeat Orders).

Margin

Sales AmountWhat the pieces were billed for after discount (POS invoice amount less discount).
Purchase AmountWhat the same pieces cost, from the purchase invoice, consignment or allocated stock piece (see the rules above).
MarginSales Amount minus Purchase Amount.
Margin %Margin divided by Sales Amount of the same pieces.
Margin / Sq FtMargin divided by the store's area. All Stores uses the area of the stores that have margin data (Malleshwaram has none).

Pre-order bills and brand-new items often have no purchase record yet, so their Margin shows "—" until they do.

In-store scans

ScansItems scanned on the POS, per store per day. About 90% of all scans are at Pune, so a low number elsewhere usually means "not scanned", not "no interest".
Scans Per Visitor / Scans Per WalkinScans divided by Walkins (visits), so someone who visits twice counts twice.

Calls (Dashboard tab)

Store Calls DialedOutbound calls made by the store's team.
Connected Calls / over 30 SecondsCalls that were answered, and those that lasted over 30 seconds.
Store TalktimeTotal time on connected calls.

Google Business Profile

GMB Strength, Google Reviews, Google RatingHow complete the listing is, and its total reviews and average star rating (current values).
Store Direction Clicks / Store Calls ClickedTaps on "Directions" and "Call" on the store's Google listing. Google reports these about 2–3 days late. Malleshwaram shows N/A until Google finishes verifying its listing.

Store operations

InventoryPieces in finished-goods stock at the store right now.
Number of StaffFixed headcount from the store config. All Stores is the sum.
Revenue Per StaffRevenue divided by the store's staff. All Stores uses the staff of the stores that have revenue data (Malleshwaram has none).

Marketing efficiency

SpendGoogle Ads plus Meta Ads spend for campaigns that name the store or city. A campaign naming several stores is shared equally; one naming only "Mumbai" or "Delhi" is shared between that city's stores.
Cost Per Session / Deal / AppointmentSpend divided by City Sessions, Deals and Meetings Raised. Because Spend is partial, these are lower-bound estimates.

Which store does a number belong to?

  • Sales, orders, revenue, margin: the store code on the sales record.
  • Walkins: the store the visit was logged at. Scans: the store of the scan.
  • Meetings, calls: the owner's store; for meetings, the Venue text if the owner is a central account.
  • Deals, website sessions: the customer's or visitor's city (Mumbai and Delhi shared between stores).
  • Spend: store or city words in the campaign name.

Good to know

  • With only some stores selected, ratio columns are worked out from the figures on screen, so they can be a point or two off for very small numbers. All Stores and a single store are exact.
  • Malleshwaram has no sales, walk-in or scan records, so those metrics show N/A for it.
  • Meeting Show and Converted marks in Zoho only started being filled in around 22 Sep 2026; meetings before that use the phone-number matching described above.
  • The Dashboard tab's table keeps the original metric list. The newer metrics are on the Store Comparison and City Wise tabs.
  • In the bill list, phone numbers are not shown.
