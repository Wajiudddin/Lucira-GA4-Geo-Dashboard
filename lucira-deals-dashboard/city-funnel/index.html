How every number is calculated

Everything comes live from BigQuery for the date range chosen on the Dashboard tab. Nothing is typed in by hand.

Data sources

  • Sessions: ds_imputed_reporting.ga4_city_sessions_daily
  • Deals: zoho_crm.cdc_deals (live Zoho feed)
  • Store Visit Raised: zoho_crm.cdc_meetings, with customer details from zoho_crm.cdc_contacts
  • Store Visited: ds_imputed_reporting.Walkin_marge
  • Orders and Revenue: ds_imputed_reporting.marketing_sale

Date range

  • Presets are Today, Yesterday (default), Last 7d, Last 30d, All data (from the first GA4 day, 3 May 2025, to today) and Custom. Last 7d and Last 30d end yesterday.
  • t-2 rule for Sessions: a selected day shows the sessions of the day before it, so Yesterday shows today minus 2 (on 15 Oct, Yesterday 14 Oct shows the sessions of 13 Oct). A range such as 1–30 Sep shows the table for 31 Aug–29 Sep. The Total Sessions card names the dates it used. Every other number uses the dates you select.
  • A yellow banner appears when a source table ends before the selected end date.

KPI cards

  • Total Sessions: the sum of the five store cities (Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Noida, Pune, Bengaluru) from the sessions table, for the dates you select.
  • Total Deals: every Zoho deal created in the range, in any city, including deals with no city.
  • City Deals: deals that have a city (every city except Unknown), with the share of Total Deals.
  • Store Visit Raised: customers with a store-visit meeting, shown with the share of Total Deals.
  • Store Visited: raised customers who checked in at a store, shown as a share of Store Visit Raised.
  • Orders: orders from those raised customers, shown as a share of Store Visit Raised. Revenue is the net sale of those orders. AOV is Revenue ÷ Orders.

Customer matching

  • A customer is matched by mobile number or email. Mobile is the last 10 digits. Email is lower-cased, and placeholder addresses ending in @lucirajewelry.com are ignored.
  • For a meeting, mobile and email come from the meeting's contact (Who_Id in zoho_crm.cdc_contacts), then from the linked deal (What_Id in cdc_deals), then from a phone number written in the contact name.
  • Walk-ins are matched on phone or email in Walkin_marge. Sales are matched on Cus_mobile / mobile1 or email / email_id in marketing_sale.
  • A meeting with no mobile and no email still counts as Store Visit Raised (by meeting id) but cannot be matched to walk-ins or orders.

Sessions

  • Source: ds_imputed_reporting.ga4_city_sessions_daily, which holds one row per day and city group (mumbai, delhi, noida, pune, bengaluru) with city_sessions. It is read for the whole selected range, so any range works, not only the last 7 days.
  • The sessions table is refreshed daily and ends about two days before today. When the selected range reaches past what the table covers, the yellow banner says so and those days show no sessions.
  • The table's city groups follow GA4's own city names, so the numbers agree with the GA4 report (Town/City). Neighbouring cities such as Pimpri-Chinchwad, Thane, Gurugram or Greater Noida are not merged into them. The table has the five store cities only, so Hyderabad and other cities have no sessions here.
  • GA4 knows the visitor's city, not a store. Mumbai sessions are split 50/50 between Borivali and Chembur, and Delhi NCR sessions 50/50 between Lajpat Nagar and Paschim Vihar (an odd session goes to the second store). Noida, Pune and Malleshwaram show their full city sessions. The split parts add back to the city total, so the Total row is not inflated.

Deals

  • Source: zoho_crm.cdc_deals, the live Zoho feed. One row per deal id, dated by created time in IST. The older Lucira_Prod.lucira_zoho_Deals table stopped on 16 Sep 2026, so it is not used.
  • A deal's city is its Address_City. Blank is shown as Unknown. Delhi districts (Central, South, East Delhi and so on), Gurgaon, Faridabad and Ghaziabad count as Delhi NCR. Gautam Buddha Nagar counts as Noida. Thane and similar places count as Mumbai. Other places count as Other Cities.
  • Zoho does not fill Store_Assigned, so deals are assigned to stores by the deal's city. Mumbai deals (including Thane and similar) are split 50/50 between Borivali and Chembur (an odd deal goes to Chembur). Delhi deals go by district: Central, South and South West Delhi, plus Gurgaon (counted as South Delhi), to Lajpat Nagar; North, North West and West Delhi to Paschim Vihar; East Delhi, Noida and Gautam Buddha Nagar to Noida. Delhi deals with only "Delhi" or "New Delhi" (and Faridabad, Ghaziabad) fit none of these districts, so they are in Total Deals and City Deals but not in a store row. Pune and Malleshwaram (Bengaluru) take their full city deals.

Store Visit Raised

  • Source: zoho_crm.cdc_meetings. Distinct customers whose first store-visit meeting was created in the range (IST). Video calls, tests, try-at-home, trial and complaint meetings are excluded.
  • The store comes from the meeting venue: Borivali, Chembur, Lajpat Nagar, Paschim Vihar, Noida, Malleshwaram or Pune, with common misspellings. Meetings at HO or with no identifiable store are not counted anywhere, so the KPI equals the store table total.
  • Meetings are not limited to deals created in the same range, so Store Visit Raised as a share of Total Deals can go above 100%.

Store Visited

  • Source: ds_imputed_reporting.Walkin_marge, StoreVisit check-ins. A raised customer counts as visited when they have a check-in on or after the meeting date. HO, Amazon and billed-return rows are excluded.
  • Store codes: BO1 Borivali, CS1 Chembur, LPN Lajpat Nagar, PV1 Paschim Vihar, N18 Noida, MAL Malleshwaram, PN1 Pune. FCS and HWI have no known city or store, so they have no store row, but a check-in there still counts a raised customer as visited.
  • A customer who raised a visit at one store and checked in at another still counts as visited, under the store of the meeting.

Orders and revenue

  • Source: ds_imputed_reporting.marketing_sale. Distinct document_no for raised customers, dated on or after their meeting. Each customer counts once, so several meetings never double-count an order or a customer.
  • Revenue is the sum of net_sale (excluding tax). AOV is revenue ÷ orders.
  • Order status: cancellations are rows where Fullfillment is cancel_order. The default view excludes them. The Order status filter can show all or only cancelled.

Store comparison chart and store table

  • The chart shows one bar per store for the metric picked in its dropdown, sorted from highest to lowest. Bar colour follows the store's city.
  • Store table columns: Sessions and Deals (assigned to stores as above), Store Visit Raised, % of Raised (the store's share of all raised), Store Visited, % Visited (Visited ÷ Raised), Orders, Order Conv. % (Orders ÷ Raised) and Revenue.
  • Visited, Orders and Revenue are dated by the meeting that started the journey, so those rates are cohort rates. Sessions, Deals and Raised use their own dates.

Filters

  • City limits every number. Store limits the store-level numbers (Store Visit Raised, Visited, Orders, Revenue and Delhi-zone deals). Sessions are not changed by the Store filter.
  • Deal source limits Deals and the meetings linked to a deal. It does not change Sessions.
  • Order source / fulfillment and Order status limit Orders and Revenue only.
  • Results are cached for 5 minutes on the web version, so a repeat query within that time returns the same numbers.
