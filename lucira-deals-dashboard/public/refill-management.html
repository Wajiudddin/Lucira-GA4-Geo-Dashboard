REFILL LOGIC

  1. SKU must have sold at least once at the same store, ever (full sales history, no date cutoff).
  2. Sales Window filter (top bar) toggles the whole dashboard between Last 90 Days and Overall (all-time); defaults to Last 90 Days.
  3. Fulfillment is RTS only.
  4. Returned and exchanged sales are ignored: a sale is skipped when its Item Line No appears in a sales return (return / return_items) or an exchange (exchange / exchange_items) dated on or after the sale. If the SKU has other sales that were not returned or exchanged it still qualifies on those; a later re-sale of the same unit counts again.
  5. Gold Coin, Silver Coin, and Silver jewelry (by item_group_name or metal) are excluded from all sales figures.
  6. Customize-order styles are excluded: COR, COE, COP/COPD, COB, COC, CNP, CTN, CNK prefixes (not real stock, made to order).
  7. Exact SKU must currently have 0 stock at that store. Store Stock = number of units of that exact SKU in the same store (the store that sold it), not other stores or HO.
  8. Store Stock and HO On Hand count only location "Finish Goods" — Repair, Vendor Return, Melting Stock, Market Place and every other location are excluded. Malleshwaram (MAL) has no Finish Goods stock rows of its own, so every SKU it sold shows Store Stock 0.
  9. SKU is matched by exact SKU + Size + Metal/Variant.
  10. Store sales are based on Billing Company (company_code), not Location.
  11. Reorder quantity is always 1.
  12. HO On Hand shows exact SKU stock at Head Office.
  13. Transfer Date = the date the stock reached the selling store. It is searched in this order, stopping at the first source that has a date (the latest one on or before the Last Sold Date): (1) stock_in_Store by store + SKU — for HWI, consignment_out, then stock_transfer, by Item Line No; (2) the same sources under the unit's previous Item Line No, when an allocation changed the line no (stock_allocation_items → stock_allocated_items); (3) branch_receipt; (4) finishing — steps 3 and 4 by Item Line No, then the previous Item Line No. Document No is the document number from the same source. Only the selling store's own transfer is used (never another store's or HO's copy of the same transfer; for steps 3 and 4 the document number must start with the selling store code; HWI uses HO documents). Both show — when no source has a date.
  14. Ageing = Last Sold Date − Transfer Date.
  15. Sales Cycle is Below 180 (≤180 days) or Above 180 (>180 days); Unknown when no qualifying transfer record exists.
  16. PO is valid only when PO Raised Date > Last Sold Date. PO validity is SKU-level only — no store-level PO linkage exists in source data.
  17. Old POs (raised before the last sale) are ignored.
  18. Network and Peer Store Availability are matched at the style level (any size/metal variant of the same style counts), not exact SKU.
  19. Network Availability requires ≥2 units at a location.
  20. Single-unit locations are excluded from Network Availability.
  21. HO, HWI Ventures, and the selling store itself are excluded from Peer Store Availability.
  22. Network/Peer Availability is shown as City-Qty(AgeingDays), e.g. "Borivali-1(254)" — city/area name, not the store code.
  23. Peer stock aged >180 days is highlighted RED (oldest unit at that store).
  24. Lifetime Sales is shown only when at least one peer stock is >180 days old.
  25. Lifetime Sales is Store + Style level and includes all sizes/metals/variants.
  26. Lifetime Sales of 1–2 is GREEN.
  27. T-1 compares today's list with the previous run.
  28. Refill Status shows TRANSFER SENT (with date) when stock_in_Store has a transfer of that exact SKU into the selling store dated after its Last Sold Date. The row stays on the list; the transfer is logged on issue, so the SKU may not yet show in store stock. This check uses stock_in_Store only, so it is not applied to HWI consignment / stock_transfer movements.
  29. PDP Views and ATC are lifetime (all dates, no date window) from GA4 (ga4_data), summed at style level — every variant/size of the style, e.g. LJ-R00034-14YWG-12 → style R00034.
  30. ATC Ratio = lifetime ATC ÷ lifetime PDP Views × 100 (style level); shown as — when there are no PDP Views.
  31. Lifetime Sale column = number of RTS sales of the exact SKU across all stores (same RTS / coin / silver / customize-order filters; returned and exchanged sales are not removed from this count).
  32. Item Line No is the ERP item_line_no of the unit on the SKU's last sale at that store (marketing_sale); it is the key used to look up Transfer Date and Document No in the consignment, stock_transfer, branch receipt and finishing tables, and to find returned / exchanged sales.
  33. Product image is matched by SKU from Shopify's own catalog where available; click the thumbnail to open it full size.
